Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Rise is built to know as little about you as possible.

The camera

The camera checks your push-ups or that you're sitting still. This happens on your phone, as it happens. No photos or video are saved, uploaded or shared.

What stays on your phone

Your settings, streaks and quiz answers stay on your phone.

This website

If you join the waitlist, we store your email and which phone you use. We use it only to tell you about Rise. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Children

Rise is not meant for children under 13.
