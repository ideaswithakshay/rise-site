Questions

Good questions.

Do I need to know how to meditate?

No. You just sit still and breathe with the circle. That's it.

Does the alarm work on silent?

Yes. Rise uses Apple's alarm system (iOS 26), the same one the Clock app uses.

Does the camera record me?

No. It only checks that your face is still. Nothing is saved or uploaded.

Is it on Android?

iPhone first. Join the waitlist and pick Android. That tells us how many people want it.
